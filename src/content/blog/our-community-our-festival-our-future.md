For more than 33 years, Otago Polyfest has been a beating heart of our community — a place where all tamariki and rangatahi can stand tall, celebrate our collective heritage and express their identity through the power of Toi Ihiihi.

This year, that heartbeat continues.

**Otago Polyfest 2026 is going ahead**, and the incredible outpouring of aroha from across our hapori has reminded us just how deeply this festival is valued.

Following the recent news that our application to the Creative New Zealand Arts Organisations and Groups Fund was unsuccessful, we have been honest about the challenges facing community-led arts organisations like ours. We believe grassroots organisations that do the heavy lifting in our communities need sustainable, long-term investment.

But we also know something powerful: Polyfest has never been built by a single organisation. **#ittakesavillage**

For 33 years, this festival has been shaped by tamariki, rangatahi, kaiako, tutors, whānau, volunteers, schools, community organisations, funders, sponsors and supporters who have all invested their time, energy and aroha.

## Keeping the beat

While we continue advocating for a funding system that recognises the true value of long-term cultural mahi, we are also taking action to strengthen the foundations of Polyfest for 2027 and beyond.

Our aim is to build a more resilient festival with a broader range of community and revenue support while keeping Polyfest accessible to our community.

Because Polyfest is more than five days on a calendar. It is a place of belonging, language, leadership and intergenerational wellbeing. It provides a stage for young people to develop confidence, celebrate culture and connect with their communities.

## Growing our community of support

There are many ways to be part of the village that keeps Polyfest going.

- **Koha:** Every contribution helps us build a stronger foundation for the future. See our [Sponsors page](/sponsors/) for bank details, or contact us if you would like to make a koha.
- **Volunteer:** Our festival relies on the mahi aroha of incredible kaimahi. Give your time, skills or energy and become part of the team behind Polyfest.
- **Support our schools:** Schools with the capacity can make a voluntary $3 contribution per student to support the festival.
- **Start a community fundraiser:** Have an idea for a fundraiser or other community initiative? We want to hear from you. If you have the energy to lead something, let's kōrero about how we can support you.
- **Stay connected:** [Email us](mailto:admin@otagopolyfest.nz) to join our mailing list and follow Otago Polyfest to hear about opportunities to get involved, future events and our journey towards a sustainable future.

## New ways to support Polyfest

As part of this work, we are also trialling a $5 entry fee for the Wednesday and Thursday evening sessions in 2026. Entry to all other sessions will remain $2 per person, per session.

This is one of the ways we are exploring how modest changes to our revenue model can help support the festival, while keeping Polyfest affordable and accessible.

## Every act of support matters

Not everyone can contribute in the same way. For some, it might be a koha. For others, it might be volunteering, buying a T-shirt, organising a fundraiser, making a school contribution, sharing our story or simply bringing your whānau along to the festival.

Together, we are not simply delivering an event. We are nurturing an ecosystem of wellbeing, leadership, culture and connection for generations to come.

Our commitment to ensuring this opportunity remains available to our young people is absolute.

Otago Polyfest is our community's cultural heartbeat. Let's keep the beat together.
